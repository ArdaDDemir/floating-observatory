# Buildings

Place structures on free island tiles (outside the core exclusion zone).

| Building | Cost focus | Buff |
|----------|------------|------|
| **Energy Core** | Crystals | +% energy per session / level |
| **Data Tower** | Energy | +% crystals per session / level |
| **Botany Lab** | Mixed | Small dual bonus |
| **Shield Gen** | Mixed | Reduces fail penalty |
| **Signal Dish** | Mixed | Stronger ambient event loot |

## Manage on the island

- **Tap / click** a placed building anytime between sessions.
- **Upgrade** raises level (visual tiers change look).
- **Relocate** moves to a free tile.
- **Destroy** refunds a portion of cost.

## Build tray

Press **B** or use the Build toolbar button to open the place tray and pick a type, then tap the island.

Economy numbers live in `src/lib/gameConfig.ts` if you fork.
