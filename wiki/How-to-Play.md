# How to Play

## Core loop

1. **Start a focus quest** — pick tag + duration (5–60m or custom).
2. **Stay with it** — ambient events may drip tiny rewards (no mid-session modals).
3. **Complete** — earn crystals & energy (buildings, streak, research multiply haul).
4. **Build** — place structures, upgrade, research, assign crew.
5. **Return tomorrow** — hit the daily goal to grow your streak.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Space | Pause / resume |
| B | Build mode tray |
| M | Lab (research, looks, crew, badges) |
| Z | Zen HUD |
| Esc | Close panels |
| **Click building** | Upgrade / relocate / destroy |
| **Click NPC** | Assign crew post |
| Touch | Orbit (1 finger), zoom (2 fingers) |

## Focus rules

- **Pause** freezes the timer.
- **Give up** applies a penalty (softened by shields / research / crew).
- Repeated fails apply a short **lockout** (not day-long bans).
- **Break** after complete is optional.

## Daily goal & streak

- Default goal: **50 minutes** / day (Settings).
- First time you hit the goal that day → streak +1 and a flat bonus.
- Streak multiplies future session rewards.
- Missing days resets streak; a gap may grant a **welcome-back** gift.
