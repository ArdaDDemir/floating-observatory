# Crew & NPCs

Crew walk on the island as tiny NPCs. Only **real roster members** appear (no fake ambient fillers).

## Slots

| Observatory level | Crew slots |
|-------------------|------------|
| 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 3 |
| … | +1 per level |
| max | **12** |

New members **join as you level** (toast + recruit SFX).

## Roles

| Role | When assigned |
|------|----------------|
| Worker | Cheaper upgrades |
| Gardener | Botany Lab bonus |
| Scout | Better event loot |
| Engineer | Energy Core / shield energy yield |
| Caretaker | Extra fail mitigation |
| Archivist | Data Tower crystals |
| Courier | Small dual bonus on any post |

## Assign posts

1. **Tap the NPC** on the island → assign panel.
2. Or **Lab → Crew** → dropdown per member.

Buffs only apply while assigned to a building.

Body styles: bot · scout · sprout · orb (by role).
